A page link is the card in the thread that stands for a page: it opens the page and keeps it findable later.

## Anatomy

- A `surface` card with a `line` border and `radius-card`. The whole card is one link.
- A `label` line: "Page", a middle dot, then its state.
- The page title in the reading face at 17px, medium weight.
- A `trace` line with one or two plain facts about the page.
- The word "Open" on the right in `accent`, wrapping below on a narrow column.

## States

| State | Label | When |
| --- | --- | --- |
| In progress | Page · in progress | The learner is still writing it |
| Kept | Page · kept | The learner has finished with it. It stays in the thread as their artifact. |

Hover washes the card with `hover`. Keyboard focus shows the `focus-ring`.

## Rules

- A page link appears once, at the point in the thread where the page was opened. Reopening the page later does not add another.
- The tutor names the page. The learner can rename it by saying so.
- At a desk, opening the link shows the page beside the conversation. On a phone it opens the page read-only.
- There is no list of pages anywhere else. To find one, the learner asks, and the tutor replies with its link.

## The consumer provides

The title, the state, the trace text and the destination.
